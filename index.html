Have you ever wondered why we usually look with our two eyes instead of three?
It is because we tend to look at what we see in the mirror, in other people, through the superficial lens - we see what is offered to us, often letting the deeper meaning pass by.
Beneath the surface lies the hidden truth, the reality, each individual is meant to discover - true lens, that we can see through. The purpose of human life itself has the power to look within, its our own choice how much of it we show to the world.
It has become the way I have chosen to live my life - from perceiving the external to discovering the internal.
I am currently studying UX/UI design at Tartu Vocational College, while also practicing and experimenting with graphic design. It is true that creativity has influenced my life in ways I would have never imagined. From a kid drawing little ponies to an expected life-long student of art and its endless possibilities.
